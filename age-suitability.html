Age Suitability

Learnzy is designed to provide a safe, educational, and age-appropriate experience for young children. We focus on simplicity, clarity, and safety in every aspect of the app.

🌈 Kid-Safe Design

  • No advertisements or third-party tracking
  • No chat, messaging, or social features
  • No user-generated content
  • Simple visuals and soft colors for comfortable viewing

🧠 Educational Focus

The app encourages meaningful screen time through:

  • Tracing lines, curves, numbers, alphabets, and shapes
  • Practicing motor skills and coordination
  • Reinforcing concepts through repetition and audio cues

🛡️ Safe and Private

  • No personal data is collected from children
  • No location access or unrestricted web browsing
  • No login or account required
